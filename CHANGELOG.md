- 2026-11-17 (maintainer: "look into the 0.8 dB at full volume"): **the 0.8 dB taken apart, and a mixer rule found.** (1) **The master volume is an integer square**, `floor(pot^2 / 256)`: the gain is flat between pot 100 and 101, 105 and 106, 112 and 113 and steps 4 then 5 units near 600, as that formula predicts (steps match to 0.0002 dB at pots 600-617); over 4468 of msfa's output it is within 0.09 dB from pot 100 up, and silent below pot 16. - 2026-11-17 (maintainer: "model the Felucca tuning offsets"): **Felucca's tuning modelled, and the mismatches explained** (`docs/FM6.md`, "The tuning model"). Measured by writing six-carrier voices into the firmware (`fm6cmp --make six:D --partials`, frequencies from a 1.5 s spectrum, about 0.02 Hz) at detune 0-14 and notes 36-108. **Two things:** (1) **the detune step depends on the note** (1.98 cents at note 36, 0.905 at 72, 0.37 at 108; msfa 0.9017 everywhere), for every operator, modulators included; (2) **the DTUN macro spreads the carriers**: 0.0945 cent a step up to 64, times 0, +1, -1, +2, -2, +3 by the carrier's place among the algorithm's carriers (modulators none). **The earlier "per-operator offsets" were a mistake of mine**: part 2 boots on the PAD preset, whose DTUN is 30, so every measurement made on it carried that spread; the preset grid used the other presets, DTUN 0. Found by turning DTUN on the screen (`fm6cmp --shot`) and measuring at DTUN 0 (exactly no spread). `fm6::Tuning::Felucca { dtun }` (default `Tuning::Msfa`, bit for bit the original; `Voice::with_tuning`, `Synth::set_tuning`; the earlier `with_op_cents` is removed): **held-out partials within 0.15 cent of the emulator, 27 carrier-modulator pairs within 0.13 cent, and the 30-run preset grid goes from a mean level spread of 7.6 dB to 0.8 dB** (BRASS SECT, SOFT PAD and TINE EP at note 84 all match; level difference a constant ~3 dB).
