## Unreleased

- 2026-11-17 (maintainer: "look into the 3 dB output gain"): **the 3 dB is the master volume.** The FM-1's pot (ADC channel 4, `Machine::pot`; the emulator starts it at 900) sets the firmware's MASTER volume, and the emulator-minus-fm6 level followed it: +0.8 dB at pot 1023, +3.0 at 900, +13.2 at 500, +29.1 at 200 (`fm6cmp --pot`), an amplitude law of about **(pot / 1070)^2** within 0.1 dB from pot 100 up, the same in Felucca, Jangada and Skiff and independent of voice, note, velocity and output level. So the "constant 3.0 dB" was the pot's default position (-2.2 dB) plus 0.8 dB at full volume. New: `fm6::felucca_master_gain(pot)` and `Synth::set_master_pot(Some(pot))` (default `None`: msfa's level, bit for bit; test added), `fm6cmp --pot` (the emulator's) and `--master` (fm6's). **With fm6's master set to the emulator's pot the level difference is within 0.2 dB at pots 150 to 1023**, and -0.1 to +0.2 dB on the five Felucca presets at the default. Not looked into: the 0.8 dB at full volume, the law below pot 60. No engine change.

- 2026-11-17 (maintainer: "look into the DRAWBARS level difference"): **the DRAWBARS level difference was the preset's distortion, not the engine.** Single operators of DRAWBARS were quieter by an amount that grew as their frequency fell and did not depend on their output level; the same plain test voice written over each of Skiff's presets was 3.3 dB quieter than usual at note 36 on **F7 only**; its FX page has **DIST 8%** (SOFT, tone 0), which the effects-off recipe (chorus, delay, reverb) had left on. With distortion also at 0 DRAWBARS and the plain voice are **+3.0 dB at every note**, as every other preset. `scripts/fm6-compare.sh` now zeroes all four; the three grids rerun: five shared presets mean level spread 0.65 dB (Felucca), 1.0 dB (Jangada), 0.65 dB (Skiff); F6-F8 all match (WOOD BARS' large "spread" is its silent tail). `fm6cmp --strip` gains `onlyK`, `ol-N`, `coarseK=V`, `fineK=V` (play one operator, lower the levels, change a ratio). No engine change. The earlier "unexplained" wording in `docs/FM6.md` and this log's previous entry is superseded.

- 2026-11-17 (maintainer: "compare Jangada and Skiff"): **fm6 compared with Jangada 1.0.1 and Skiff 2.6** (`docs/FM6.md`, "Jangada and Skiff"; `FM6_FIRMWARE=jangada|skiff scripts/fm6-compare.sh`). Both are Felucca forks with the same engine: the detune step at DTUN 0 is equal to four decimals in all three firmwares, the DTUN spread is the same (only the PAD preset, F5, has it), the output is the same 3.0 dB below fm6, and **`Tuning::Felucca` fits both without a change** (mean level spread over the five shared presets: 7.5-7.6 dB with msfa's tuning, 1.2 dB on Jangada and 0.7 dB on Skiff with it). Their F6-F8 presets: NYLON PICK and WOOD BARS match; **DRAWBARS is 1.0-3.1 dB quieter in the emulator than the usual 3 dB, unexplained** (not the carrier count; stripping the voice's features changes nothing). Jangada's note-on latency is 16 ms (7-9 ms in the others). **Reaching FM6 differs**: Skiff boots part 2 on it; Jangada boots on its older four-operator DIGITAL engine and its FM6 presets are 55 clicks down the list, and a big encoder turn is not taken one for one, so `fm6cmp --encn NAME:N` turns one click at a time. Same caveats: the emulator, one note at a time, not the stock firmware or a unit.

- 2026-11-17 (maintainer: "model the Felucca tuning offsets"): **Felucca's tuning modelled, and the mismatches explained** (`docs/FM6.md`, "The tuning model"). Measured by writing six-carrier voices into the firmware (`fm6cmp --make six:D --partials`, frequencies from a 1.5 s spectrum, about 0.02 Hz) at detune 0-14 and notes 36-108. **Two things:** (1) **the detune step depends on the note** (1.98 cents at note 36, 0.905 at 72, 0.37 at 108; msfa 0.9017 everywhere), for every operator, modulators included; (2) **the DTUN macro spreads the carriers**: 0.0945 cent a step up to 64, times 0, +1, -1, +2, -2, +3 by the carrier's place among the algorithm's carriers (modulators none). **The earlier "per-operator offsets" were a mistake of mine**: part 2 boots on the PAD preset, whose DTUN is 30, so every measurement made on it carried that spread; the preset grid used the other presets, DTUN 0. Found by turning DTUN on the screen (`fm6cmp --shot`) and measuring at DTUN 0 (exactly no spread). `fm6::Tuning::Felucca { dtun }` (default `Tuning::Msfa`, bit for bit the original; `Voice::with_tuning`, `Synth::set_tuning`; the earlier `with_op_cents` is removed): **held-out partials within 0.15 cent of the emulator, 27 carrier-modulator pairs within 0.13 cent, and the 30-run preset grid goes from a mean level spread of 7.6 dB to 0.8 dB** (BRASS SECT, SOFT PAD and TINE EP at note 84 all match; level difference a constant ~3 dB). Fitted to measurements of Felucca 1.8.2 (no source read), ratio mode only, 44.1 kHz; not the stock firmware or a unit. New: `fm6cmp --peaks`, `--partials`, `--shot`, `--dtun`, `--tuning`; `Tuning` tests.

- 2026-11-17 (maintainer: "look at the editor protocol"): **voices written into the firmware, and the tuning difference found.** Felucca's editor protocol (SysEx over USB-MIDI; only `web/EDITOR_PROTOCOL.md` was read) has `FM6_PUT`, which plays a 128-byte voice on a track at once. **The core now exposes the emulator's USB host** (`Machine::usb_plug`, `usb_midi_send` (bounded: 4 KiB a message, 64 KiB queued), `usb_midi_take`, `usb_product`; opt-in; test added). `fm6cmp --make KIND` writes a purpose-made voice (one carrier on algorithm 32, or one feature) and plays the same bytes on fm6; `--put`, `--strip`, `--beat`, `--op-cents` and `--no-lfo` / `--no-peg` isolate parts of a real voice. **Findings:** envelopes (slow, rising, multi-stage), modulation, feedback, ratios, velocity and key scaling match fm6 one at a time (constant 3.0 dB gain); **tuning does not**: Felucca's detune step is larger on low notes (7 steps = 10.6, 9.2, 7.55, 6.3 cents at C4-C7 against fm6's 6.29) and its operators 4, 5, 6 sit +5.65, -5.65, +8.5 cents off operator 1 at centre detune. That explains SOFT PAD (with detune stripped and the offsets added to fm6: spread 12.9 to 2.5 dB) but the offsets worsen TINE EP and BRASS SECT, so they are an experiment (`fm6::Voice::with_op_cents`, `Synth::set_op_cents`, default none; test added), not a model. The earlier LFO/ENV/pitch-envelope/envelope-timing hypotheses are ruled out. Felucca's engine, one note at a time, not the stock firmware or a unit.

- 2026-11-17 (maintainer: "set the LFO and ENV neutral and rerun"): **the LFO and ENV pages made neutral, and the grid rerun: no change.** Felucca's own LFO and ENV pages were already neutral for these presets (destinations 0%, sync off, mod matrix off, amp ENV 1 ms, SUS 100%); pushing every control on them to its minimum moved BRASS SECT and SOFT PAD by 0.1 dB or less. `fm6cmp --no-lfo --no-peg` (the voice's own vibrato and pitch envelope made neutral on the fm6 side) changed nothing either, so the mismatches are not the LFO or the pitch envelope; the suspect is the operator envelopes of the slow and rising voices (unverified). `docs/FM6.md` and `ROADMAP.md` say so; the earlier hypothesis there is struck.
