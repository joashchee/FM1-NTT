- 2026-11-16 (maintainer's brief: bundle the recommended, popular and permissive firmwares): surveyed the reference kit, awesome-fm-1 (222 firmwares), the Firmware Hub, a ranked guide and the press (Reddit could not be searched); **the popular ones are GPL-3.0 or closed, so only Haunted FM-1 v0.1 qualifies and is bundled**: five firmwares by David Ryan (MIT, on MPL-2.0 platform layers, Apache-2.0 JieLi files, OFL fonts), unmodified, in `bundled-firmware/haunted-fm1/` with `MANIFEST.tsv` (hashes), licence texts, `SOURCES.md`, `NOTICE`; `scripts/check-bundled.py` (9 tests) enforces the policy in `check.sh` and the package build; `docs/FIRMWARE-CATALOG.md` records the survey and why each popular firmware is out; the FILES panel lists them (B), the `.deb` installs them, Rec demo / the benchmark / the report use UNTRUE STEP by default, the self-test boots every one. **First real firmware through the emulator: all five boot and draw; UNTRUE STEP runs at 1.00x and makes sound; on an M1 WORN TAPE runs 0.90x real time interpreted and 5.12x with the native JIT.** A real firmware exposed a bug (restoring a flash slot halted the machine): fixed to write only changed sectors, with a regression test.
- 2026-11-16 (maintainer: GPL bundling, "follow all your recommendations and put on roadmap"): **two firmware tiers**. `firmware-pack/` is a separate copyleft pack: **Felucca 1.8.2, FoMni-1 0.4.1 and Jangada 1.0.1** (GPL-3.0-only with MIT, MPL-2.0, Apache-2.0, CC0-1.0, OFL-1.1 parts), each unmodified with its **complete corresponding source** (27 MB), licence texts, `SOURCES.md` (with a written offer) and `NOTICE`, chosen after reading each one's own licence files; **SLOOP, Melodee and X0X were checked and left out** (reserved icon atlas, Casio and Sequential sound data, TR-909 sample provenance), reasons in `docs/FIRMWARE-CATALOG.md`. `scripts/check-bundled.py` (16 tests) enforces both tiers, the source hashes, no GPL in the permissive tier, and a `--no-pack APP_DIR` mode for the Mac App Store build; `r36s/build-deb.sh --pack` builds `FM1-NTT-firmware-pack.deb` (hash-verified), and the app's package is built with a guard that it holds nothing from the pack; the FILES panel marks the tiers B and G; the self-test boots all eight bundled firmwares.
